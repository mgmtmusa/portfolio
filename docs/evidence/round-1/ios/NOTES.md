# Real iPhone Safari (iOS 27.2 Simulator, iPhone 17), 24 November

Served from the local production build (`astro preview`), captured with `xcrun simctl io screenshot`.

1. **Found and fixed: the knock-out hid the name and portrait.** `iphone17-rest.png` (first capture, against the build before the fix) shows the masthead and portrait invisible everywhere except under the slab. The bend's two-layer mask (`mask-composite: exclude` / `-webkit-mask-composite: xor`) is mis-composited by iOS Safari. Playwright's WebKit and mobile-safari projects did not catch it. `src/scripts/bend.ts` now builds the knock-out from additive layers only (four bands around the hole and four radial corner pieces, no mask-composite) and clears it when the hole misses an element. `iphone17-rest-fixed.png` shows the name, portrait and bend correct. Hero specs after the fix: 98 passed, 34 skipped, 0 failed (chromium, webkit, mobile-chrome, mobile-safari); `astro check` 0 errors.
2. **Open, for the creative director: the phone portrait edge shows against the light field.** Sampled at 3x in `iphone17-rest-fixed.png`: just outside the portrait's right edge the page reads about 22,21,20 (the light field lifts it above #121110), while the portrait just inside reads 18,16,15 to 21,19,18. The step is about 1 level near the top (y 450) and about 4 levels near the bottom (y 1200), so a faint vertical box edge is visible at CSS x about 338. The likely cause is the phone frame cropping the 351p image at 280p, which cuts inside the image's own dissolve. Desktop not yet sampled the same way.
3. `iphone17-scrolled.png` (before the fix, anchor `#path-h`): the dock sits above Safari's floating toolbar and the strip's scroll-edge mask works.
